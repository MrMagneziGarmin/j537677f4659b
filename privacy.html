Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

WOD Journal is a fitness tracking application for CrossFit and functional fitness athletes. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

What data we collect

  • Workout data synced from connected devices: activities, heart rate, sleep, stress, and body battery
  • Workout logs, personal records, and notes you enter manually
  • Basic account information (email address) for authentication

How we use it

  • To display your fitness data within the app
  • To generate coaching insights and recovery recommendations
  • To enable community features such as leaderboards and shared workouts, if you opt in

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your data to anyone
  • We do not share your data with advertisers
  • We do not use your data for purposes other than operating this app

Third-party services

  • Garmin Connect API — used to sync your device data. Garmin's own privacy policy governs data on their platform.
  • AI coaching — an AI provider may process aggregated workout summaries to generate coaching insights. No raw device data is sent to the AI provider. This feature requires your explicit opt-in.

Data storage and security

Your data is stored securely. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
